# Imran Ansari

### Postdoctoral Researcher | Network Science | Mathematical & Computational Finance | Data Science

I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, India**, working on mathematical and computational approaches to complex financial systems.

I hold a **Ph.D. in Mathematics** from Shiv Nadar Institution of Eminence, where my research focused on **network science, mesoscopic structures, and financial networks**.

My research lies at the intersection of **Network Science, Mathematical Finance, Computational Finance, and Data Science**, with particular interests in:

- **Financial Networks & Systemic Risk**
- **Core–Periphery and Community Structures**
- **Random Matrix Theory**
- **Portfolio Optimization & Risk Management**
- **Network-based Financial Modeling**
- **Statistical and Information-Theoretic Methods**
- **Computational Methods for Complex Systems**

I am particularly interested in understanding how **structural organization, collective dynamics, and statistical dependencies in financial markets** can be translated into practical tools for portfolio construction, risk assessment, and systemic-risk analysis.

My methodological toolkit includes **Random Matrix Theory, network filtering, spectral methods, optimization, statistical modeling, and computational simulations**.
